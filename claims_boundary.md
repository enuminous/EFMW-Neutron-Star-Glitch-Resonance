# Claims Boundary

## Mathematically proved here

For the waveform

\[
f(t)=A e^{-t/\tau}\sin(\pi t/\tau)
\]

on its first positive lobe:

- the unique peak occurs at \(t=(\tau/\pi)\arctan\pi\);
- the normalized peak time is about 0.401907;
- the normalized peak amplitude is about 0.638.

## Proposed observational model

A permanent glitch step plus a damped resonant transient is proposed as a model to compare with pulsar timing data.

## Not proved here

This repository does not prove that:

- neutron decay causes pulsar glitches;
- pulsar glitches are universally \(\pi\)-resonant;
- the fitted damping timescale is a neutron lifetime;
- the model outperforms standard vortex-based glitch models;
- any specific pulsar data set supports the hypothesis.

Those are empirical and microphysical questions requiring independent tests.
